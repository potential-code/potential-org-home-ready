Our story

New foundation. Twenty-year track record.

Potential.org Foundation was set up in December 2025, but its work started long before. Since 2005, Potential.com — a social enterprise — has designed and delivered empowerment programs for governments, global brands and NGOs: youth innovation challenges, women's entrepreneurship, SME growth and financial literacy.

Those programs were funded by sponsors and usually limited to one country, one cohort or one season. The Foundation exists to make them free and open to anyone, with the same team, the same platform and the same methods — now amplified by AI.

  1. 2005
    Potential.com is founded

    A social enterprise designing practical, impactful development programs around the world.

  2. 2010
    SMEEP launches

    The SME Empowerment Program — free for entrepreneurs — goes on to train 300,000+ SME managers.

  3. 2012
    10,000+ entrepreneurs in 29 countries

    Free live webinars in four languages and free events in ten cities.

  4. 2013
    Hadafi launches

    Women's entrepreneurship, first in Saudi Arabia, then across MENA — 100,000+ women since.

  5. 2018
    Tatawwar with HSBC

    Youth social entrepreneurship on the UN SDGs, later rolled out across seven countries.

  6. 2025
    Potential.org Foundation

    Incorporated as a nonprofit in Delaware on 16 December 2025.

  7. 2026
    Free, AI-powered, for everyone

    Programs, three pillar guides and three learning games — open to all.
